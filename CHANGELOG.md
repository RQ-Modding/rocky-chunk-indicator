# Changelog

Each section is a version of the mod, the `version` in `mod.json`. The release workflow copies the
section of a tagged version into the release notes.

## 1.0.0

First version, from a player's suggestion.
- A Rocky Chunk mark at the top left of each character's status page: faded until the character
  consumed the chunk's power (Rock On's 10 skill points), full after; its tip says which.
- Putting a Rocky Chunk on a character who already consumed it asks first: another one gives no
  skill points. No is the default.
